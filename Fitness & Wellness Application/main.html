Proper Squat Form

Squats are a fundamental lower body exercise that targets your glutes, quads, and hamstrings. Proper form is essential to prevent injury and maximize effectiveness.

Steps:

  1. Stand with feet shoulder-width apart
  2. Keep your chest up and back straight
  3. Lower your body as if sitting in a chair
  4. Go as low as comfortable while keeping heels on the ground
  5. Push through your heels to return to starting position
